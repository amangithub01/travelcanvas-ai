## Message to an administrator

> Hi, I am developing an early travel-planning prototype and researching how
> people currently plan short trips within India. May I share one invitation for
> a voluntary, unpaid 20-minute interview with adults who personally planned a
> 2 to 4 night solo domestic trip in the past six months? This is startup research,
> not an academic study or a sales call. No bookings, payment details, or marketing
> signup are involved. I will follow your community rules and will not message
> members individually without their invitation.

If permission is denied or unanswered, do not post anyway. Do not imply organizer
approval unless explicitly granted.

## Invitation after approval

> Have you personally planned a 2 to 4 night solo trip within India in the past
> six months? I am developing an early travel-planning prototype and would like
> to understand your recent planning experience in a voluntary, unpaid
> 20-minute conversation. This is research, not a sales call; there is no need
> to use the app, share booking documents, or provide payment details.
> If interested, reply privately through this channel. Please do not post
> personal trip details publicly.

Add specific times only when you can actually attend. Use your existing private
messaging channel to schedule; do not collect contacts in the repository or
require an external form.

## Private screener

Ask only after someone opts in:

1. Are you 18 or older? Do not ask for an ID document or exact birth date.
2. Did you take a solo trip within India in the past six months?
3. Was it 2 to 4 nights?
4. Did you personally organize the itinerary and transport or accommodation,
   rather than having the entire trip arranged for you?
5. Are you comfortable discussing that experience for 20 minutes without sharing
   sensitive information?

Someone outside this audience may still provide exploratory learning, but do
not count that session in the primary sample. Budget one interview per person;
do not count their later usability session as a second independent interview.

## Today's finish line

- Request permission through up to three relevant channels you can legitimately
  access. Ask warm contacts for introductions, not contact lists.
- Aim to book the first two eligible interviews, then continue toward 8 to 10.
  This is a target, not a promised response rate.
- Use participant IDs such as P01 only after opt-in. Keep scheduling contacts
  separate from anonymized notes. Follow the kit's consent rules before recording
  interview responses.
- Record channel-level counts privately: permission requested, permission
  granted, opt-ins, eligible volunteers, and bookings. Zero responses are data;
  do not fill the trackers with invented participants.

Do not reveal the product's features before the past-trip interview. Follow
[interview.md](interview.md) once a real participant is available.
